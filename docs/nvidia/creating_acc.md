**Step 1 : Open the [NVIDIA site](https://build.nvidia.com/)**

**Step 2 : Go to the login page & create your account**

**Step 3 : For the cloud account name, give a name to your account**

**Step 4 : Tada ! ✨ you've created your NVIDIA NIM account**
